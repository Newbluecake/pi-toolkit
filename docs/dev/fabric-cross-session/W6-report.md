## Deviations / findings (for the verifier)

1. **First terminal snapshot can predate `outcome`** (found by the real-stack test: the reducer/adapter publishes two terminal copies, the first without `outcome`). `formatWakeResult` therefore reads `outcome.text ?? diag.text ?? error`. Pinned by a unit test and the e2e test.
2. **Parked-then-carried `wake:true` senders are not subscribed** (a record parked by revive downgrade keeps `wake:true`, but is later carried by a human resume's inbox block, not by a redirect). Plan §3.4d only names the trigger and coalesced (redirect) senders; left as is.
3. **Not done (outside the scope list you gave; plan W6 row mentions them): `/agent status` wake line and `get_subagent_result` "Woken by … / +N inbox messages" text** (plan §7). `WakeService.stats()` gained `wakeResults`/`wakeResultsSkipped` for it. Suggest folding into gate G or a W6b.
4. Subscribing to a successor whose snapshot is neither live nor in the replayed log (evicted) leaves its entry unsettled until `dispose`; entries are ≤ 8 wakers and only exist for revived runs. A tombstone-based minimal summary was judged over-engineering.
5. `prettier --write src` was run once by mistake during the work (it only rewrote files that were not Prettier-clean at that instant, i.e. other agents' in-progress edits under `src/web-hub/ui` / `src/ask-user`); `format:check` is clean. I did not touch `src/ask-user` by hand.
6. A temporary typecheck failure `src/ask-user/index.ts(578)` (other agent r_H0XA5N7F mid-edit) made `tests/service/request-threading` (compile gate) fail once; it cleared when their edit settled.
